# Veil Ops — design lock

## Fantasy

You are an **imperial intelligence officer**. On the **overworld you are a little sprite** walking a sector map (FF-style). When contact happens, the game shifts into **3D over-the-shoulder turn-based combat** with real models. Large fights arrive in **waves**.

Inspired by the *structure* of Final Fantasy overworld → battle (not a Square Enix remake). Original setting only.

## Pillars

1. **Ops fantasy** — briefs, standing, debriefs matter as much as shooting.
2. **OTS combat framing** — camera anchors to where you were when the fight started.
3. **Action-point turns** — move, shoot, reload, skills, gear, and cover each cost AP; enemies use the same rules.
3b. **Directional cover** — half → full strength soaks frontal fire; flanking punishes camping.
4. **Waves** — enemy count splits into manageable turns.
5. **Hybrid presentation** — sprites for travel; 3D models only in combat dioramas.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Multiplayer
- Full ATB / real-time dodge
- Giant seamless overworld
- Voice acting / cinematic timelines
