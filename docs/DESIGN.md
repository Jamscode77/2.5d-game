# Veil Ops — design lock

## Fantasy

You are an **imperial intelligence officer**. Travel a small sci-fi **overworld**, take ops for the empire, and when contact happens the game shifts into **2.5D over-the-shoulder turn-based combat**. Large fights arrive in **waves**.

Inspired by the *structure* of Final Fantasy overworld → battle (not a Square Enix remake). Original setting only.

## Pillars

1. **Ops fantasy** — briefs, standing, debriefs matter as much as shooting.
2. **OTS combat framing** — camera anchors to where you were when the fight started.
3. **Pure turn-based v1** — semi/ATB later if desired.
4. **Waves** — enemy count splits into manageable turns.
5. **2.5D stages** — shallow diorama spaces, not open-world 3D.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Multiplayer
- Full ATB / real-time dodge
- Giant seamless overworld
- Voice acting / cinematic timelines
