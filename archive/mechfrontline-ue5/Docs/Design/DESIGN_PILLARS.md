# Mech Frontline — Design Pillars

Single-player tactical mobile-frame combat inspired by the *feel* of mission-based mech ops (slow, deliberate, squad-aware) — **not** a clone of any licensed title. All names, factions, and suits are original.

## Fantasy

You are a frame pilot attached to a forward operations unit. Missions are short, objective-driven sorties across industrial / urban / canyon battlefields. You fly one frame, command a small allied wing, and manage heat, armor zones, and target priority.

## Pillars

1. **Weight** — Frames feel heavy. Acceleration and turning are deliberate; boost is a scarce resource.
2. **Tactical targeting** — Soft lock, zone damage, and loadout choice matter more than twitch aim alone.
3. **Squad presence** — 2–3 allies respond to Hold / Follow / Attack My Target. You are a leader, not a lone wolf.
4. **Mission clarity** — Every sortie has a brief, primary objectives, and a clean success / fail state.
5. **Readable silhouette** — Low-to-mid poly frames, strong faction colors, HUD that surfaces hull / heat / lock / objectives.

## Non-goals (v1 scaffold)

- Multiplayer / PvP
- Base builder / hangar economy simulation
- Open world
- Licensed IP, mech names, or faction marks from existing franchises
- Full Behavior Tree combat AI (stub controllers only)

## Reference tone (gameplay only)

Think: third-person mech, mission select → briefing → sortie → debrief. Weapons overheat. Allies can be ordered. Maps are authored arenas with spawners and objective volumes — not procedural planets.
